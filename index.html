About us

Sewa Bath Solution is a supplier of bathroom fittings, sanitaryware and plumbing hardware based in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. We work directly with brands like Prayag and PAPE to bring genuine, quality-checked fittings, faucets, cisterns, pipes and accessories to plumbers, contractors and retailers across the region.

From brass extension nipples and CPVC piping to wall mixers, pillar cocks and complete flush systems, our catalog covers what a working plumbing site actually needs, with real stock ready to move rather than a wait on far-off suppliers.

We built this business on straightforward dealings: honest pricing and fast turnaround, whether you're fitting out a single bathroom or sourcing for a multi-unit project. Reach us on WhatsApp or phone for stock checks, pricing or product recommendations.
